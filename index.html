connect a Google Sheet so this dashboard stays the same everywhere you open it

Paste the Web App URL from your deployed Google Apps Script (see the setup guide I gave you). Once connected, adding, editing, or deleting a flight here updates your Google Sheet — and any device with this URL sees the same data.

"Push" uploads everything currently shown on this device and overwrites the Sheet — use this once, from the device that has your complete data. After that, just use "Pull" (or reload the page) on other devices to catch up. This page also auto-pulls whenever you switch back to it.
Backup & restore
everything you add/edit/delete lives only in this browser — back it up regularly
Restoring replaces your added flights, edits, and deletions with the contents of the backup file. The original 409-flight log itself always stays intact in the file.
Share this report
a read-only snapshot, safe to send to anyone — no editing tools, no personal storage
"Download shareable report" generates a standalone HTML file you can email or hand off. "Print full dashboard" opens your browser's print dialog with the whole dashboard formatted for paper — choose "Save as PDF" as the destination to keep it for your records.
